About AmpFootballFit

  • 12 weeks, 3 phases: Base, Load, Sport. Each phase is 4 weeks, building up from week to week, with an easier 4th week to recover.
  • Reps first, then a harder level: within a level you build up the reps (for example from 3 × 10 to 3 × 20). Reach the top of the range in 2 sessions in a row (3 for jumps) without it feeling too hard, and the app suggests the harder version, where you start again at the low end. Too hard suggests an easier one. You decide.
  • Pain rule: tiredness and burning muscles are fine. Stop an exercise if pain goes above 6 out of 10. Full rules: Program tab, "Safety and match days".
  • Your data stays on this phone. Make a backup now and then in Settings.
  • No technique coaching: ball and crutch skills come from your coach. This app does conditioning only, and it does not replace your physio or prosthetist.

Safety and match days
  • Pain rule: tiredness and burning muscles are normal. Stop the exercise if pain goes above 6 out of 10. Take a rest day and try again; if the pain stays, see your physio or doctor.
  • Check your residual limb: stop and contact your prosthetist or physio if you see red pressure marks that don't fade straight away, feel a local pain point that appears suddenly, or have swelling that doesn't go down with the leg raised or with ice.
  • Jumps and hops have the highest impact. Check with your physio before you start them, and practise with crutches, a partner or a wall bar for support until your balance is safe.
  • Day before a matchday: no hard training and no jumps. Light stretching and activation only.
  • Day after a matchday: light or unloaded exercise, such as swimming.
  • After week 12: keep doing the program to maintain your level, and keep building reps or weight where you can.

Based on a review by a physiotherapist (November 2026). It does not replace advice from your own physio, doctor or prosthetist.

Skills

What this tab is for: a place to keep the football-technique drills your coach or physio gives you — things like crutch turns, striking balance, or dribbling control. AmpFootballFit doesn't teach technique itself, so this starts mostly empty and you fill it in.

For each drill: paste a video link (from your coach, or one you found), note who showed it to you or verified it, and tap Mark completed each time you practise it so you can see what you're actually working on.

A few common drill names are listed under each heading as starting points — edit or delete them freely, or add your own with + Add custom drill.
